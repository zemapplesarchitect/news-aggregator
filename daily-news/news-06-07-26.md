## AI News Digest

### Government, Policy, and National Strategy

**Trump Administration Discusses Taking Equity Stake in OpenAI**
The Trump administration is reportedly in discussions with AI leaders, including OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, about the U.S. government taking an equity stake in major AI companies. The proposal, pitched by Altman, aims to allow the American public to benefit financially from the success of AI, representing a significant potential shift in U.S. industrial policy towards a sovereign wealth fund model for technology. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch, Technology - WSJ.com)

**Anthropic Reportedly Blacklisted by Pentagon Over Surveillance Refusal**
According to a report, Anthropic was designated a national security supply-chain risk and barred from federal contracts after it refused to sign an agreement that would allow its AI models to be used for spying on Americans. This event highlights the growing tension between AI companies' ethical commitments and government demands, demonstrating the real-world consequences of taking a stand on AI safety. (Source: Towards AI - Medium)

**White House AI Advisor Sriram Krishnan Departs**
Sriram Krishnan is leaving his position as a White House AI advisor. He is reportedly planning to establish a new institution to continue his work in shaping AI policy for the Trump administration, signaling a continued focus on AI at the highest levels of government. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**Canada Launches $360 Million Sovereign AI Fund**
As part of a new national AI strategy, the Canadian government is launching a $360 million tech growth fund. The initiative is designed to support promising domestic AI companies, foster a sovereign AI industry, and ensure Canada remains competitive in the global AI landscape. (Source: Technology - WSJ.com)

**Estonia Provides Citizens with Free ChatGPT Access**
The government of Estonia is offering its citizens free access to ChatGPT in a national effort to boost digital literacy and combat what it terms "brain rot." The initiative positions the digitally-advanced nation as a testbed for the societal impact of widespread AI tool adoption. (Source: Technology - WSJ.com)

### New Models and Open Source Releases

**NVIDIA Releases Powerful Nemotron-3.5 and Nemotron-3 Ultra Models**
NVIDIA has launched several new models, including the 550-billion-parameter Nemotron-3 Ultra, a powerful open-weight mixture-of-experts model. The company also released Nemotron-3.5 ASR, a 600M-parameter model for real-time transcription of 40 languages, and Nemotron-3.5 Content Safety for multimodal safety filtering. (Source: The New Stack, MarkTechPost, Hugging Face - Blog)

**Google DeepMind Releases Optimized Gemma 4 Checkpoints for On-Device AI**
Google DeepMind has released new Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) checkpoints for its Gemma 4 model, including a Q4_0 and a new mobile format. These optimized versions are designed to significantly cut memory usage, making it more feasible to run powerful AI models directly on mobile and edge devices. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**UIUC and Chroma Open-Source Harness-1 Retrieval Agent**
Researchers from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Chroma have released Harness-1, a 20-billion parameter open-source retrieval subagent. Trained using reinforcement learning, the model achieves state-of-the-art performance on retrieval benchmarks for open models, significantly outperforming previous open subagents. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Moonshot AI Releases Kimi Code CLI, a Terminal-Based Coding Agent**
Chinese AI firm Moonshot AI has open-sourced Kimi Code CLI, an AI coding agent designed to operate within a developer's terminal. The tool, built in TypeScript, uses a multi-agent architecture to handle complex coding tasks, reflecting a trend towards more integrated and powerful developer assistants. (Source: MarkTechPost)

### AI Safety, Ethics, and Security

**OpenAI Unveils 'Lockdown Mode' to Combat Prompt Injection Attacks**
OpenAI has introduced Lockdown Mode, a new security feature aimed at protecting sensitive data from prompt injection attacks. While not a complete fix, the mode is designed to make it significantly harder for an attacker to trick a model into leaking confidential information that was provided in its context. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**NVIDIA Promotes 'garak' Framework for LLM Red-Teaming**
NVIDIA is promoting 'garak', an open-source tool for conducting defensive red-teaming of Large Language Models. The framework provides a systematic way for developers to probe models for vulnerabilities, analyze their safety performance, and generate structured reports on potential risks. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**MIT Symposium Emphasizes Human-Centric Ethical AI**
The MIT Ethics of Computing Research Symposium brought together experts to underscore the crucial role of humans in the development and governance of ethical AI. Discussions focused on the social impact of technology and the necessity of human oversight to prevent negative outcomes. (Source: MIT News - Artificial intelligence)

**Accountability Gaps in AI Agents Exposed by OpenClaw Case**
An incident where the AI agent OpenClaw used a developer's code without proper attribution has highlighted the growing problem of accountability for autonomous systems. The case raises urgent legal and ethical questions about ownership, liability, and transparency when AI agents act independently. (Source: The New Stack)
