### AI Governance, Policy, and National Security

**Trump Scraps AI Executive Order After Pressure from Tech Leaders**
President Donald Trump has postponed signing a planned executive order on AI safety, citing concerns it could hinder the U.S.'s competitive edge against China. The decision reportedly came after last-minute appeals from tech figures including Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, and investor David Sacks, who argued the proposed regulations and pre-release government security reviews were overly restrictive and could "have been a blocker" to innovation. The order had been delayed multiple times before being scrapped. (Source: AI News, TechCrunch, Technology - WSJ.com)

**US Government Takes $2 Billion Equity Stake in Quantum Computing Firms**
The U.S. government is investing $2 billion in nine quantum computing companies, taking equity stakes in a strategic move to bolster national capabilities in the field. IBM is set to receive the largest share at $1 billion, with other beneficiaries including Rigetti and GlobalFoundries. The move signifies a major federal commitment to advancing quantum technology, which is seen as critical for future economic and national security. (Source: Biz &amp; IT - Ars Technica, Technology - WSJ.com)

OpenAI is opening its first Applied AI Lab outside the U.S. in Singapore, as part of a new "OpenAI for Singapore" initiative backed by a S$300 million commitment. This partnership highlights Singapore's ambition to become a global hub for AI development and governance. (Source: AI News)

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed an executive order focused on the impact of artificial intelligence on the state's workforce. (Source: Technology - WSJ.com)

### New AI Models and Research

**Microsoft Releases Fara1.5, a Family of Advanced Browser Agents**
Microsoft Research has released Fara1.5, a family of open-weight browser automation agents available in 4B, 9B, and 27B parameter sizes. The largest model, Fara1.5-27B, achieved a 72% score on the Online-Mind2Web benchmark, outperforming competitors like OpenAI's Operator and Google's Gemini 2.5 Computer Use. The release also includes FaraGen1.5, the synthetic data pipeline used for training the agents. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Alibaba's Qwen Team Unveils Qwen3.7-Max with 1M-Token Context**
At the 2026 Alibaba Cloud Summit, the Qwen team introduced Qwen3.7-Max, its most advanced reasoning agent model to date. The model boasts a 1-million-token context window and an "extended-thinking" mode designed for complex, long-horizon tasks like coding and workflow automation. It achieved a top-five ranking among proprietary models on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Cohere Releases Command A+, a 218B Sparse MoE Model**
Cohere has open-sourced Command A+, a powerful 218B parameter Sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model designed for agentic workflows. The model, which supports 48 languages and includes multimodal reasoning capabilities, is notable for its efficiency, capable of running on as few as two H100 GPUs with quantization. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**ByteDance Releases Lance, a Unified Multimodal Model**
ByteDance's Intelligent Creation Lab has released Lance, an open-source model that unifies image and video understanding, generation, and editing within a single framework. The model is highly efficient, using only 3 billion activated parameters to perform tasks across all three modalities. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Nvidia and Hugging Face Detail Nemotron-Labs Diffusion Language Models**
Nvidia is developing Nemotron-Labs Diffusion Language Models, a new architecture aiming for "speed-of-light" text generation. This approach applies diffusion techniques, commonly used in image generation, to language modeling, potentially offering a new paradigm for creating text with different trade-offs in speed and quality. (Source: Hugging Face - Blog)

**Nous Research Releases Contrastive Neuron Attribution (CNA)**
Nous Research has introduced Contrastive Neuron Attribution (CNA), a novel technique for steering the behavior of Large Language Models. The method identifies and ablates specific neuron circuits within the model to control outputs without requiring retraining, weight modification, or the use of sparse autoencoders, preserving general performance. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**xAI Enhances Grok with Persistent "Skills" and Improved Tool Use**
xAI has released "Grok Skills" and updated the API for its Grok 4.3 model, enabling it to learn and retain custom expertise across conversations. This update allows developers to imbue the model with persistent knowledge and specialized abilities, making it more effective for dedicated tasks. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

### AI Infrastructure, Developer Tools &amp; Security

**The Rise of the "Forward Deployed Engineer" in Enterprise AI**
A new, highly sought-after role, the "Forward Deployed Engineer" (FDE), is becoming central to enterprise AI strategy, pioneered by Palantir and now adopted by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. FDEs work on-site with clients to build custom solutions, a model seen as essential for deploying complex AI systems that standard SaaS products cannot handle. This trend is driving major investments, including OpenAI's $4B+ Deployment Company. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Perplexity Open-Sources "Bumblebee" Supply-Chain Scanner**
AI search company Perplexity has open-sourced Bumblebee, an internal security tool used to protect its developer endpoints. Bumblebee is a read-only inventory collector for macOS and Linux that scans for vulnerabilities in dependencies like npm, PyPI, and browser extensions without running any code, helping to secure the software supply chain. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Hacker Group "TeamPCP" Poisoning Open Source Code at Unprecedented Scale**
A hacker group known as TeamPCP is conducting a large-scale software supply chain attack, compromising and poisoning numerous open-source code repositories. GitHub has been a recent target of the group's widespread campaign, which poses a significant threat to developers and organizations who rely on open-source packages. (Source: Biz &amp; IT - Ars Technica)

**Cloudflare Completes Six-Layer Agent Infrastructure Stack**
Cloudflare has rebuilt its Browser Run service on its own Containers platform, achieving 4x higher concurrency and 50% faster response times. This upgrade completes a comprehensive six-layer infrastructure stack for building AI agents, encompassing compute, orchestration, memory, browsing, and commerce capabilities. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Y Combinator's Garry Tan's "GBrain" Offers Self-Wiring Memory for AI Agents**
GBrain, an open-source memory layer developed by Y Combinator's Garry Tan, provides a solution for AI agent amnesia. It uses a markdown-based knowledge graph that automatically "wires" itself through regex inference, creating persistent memory for agents without relying on expensive LLM calls. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Technical Guides Emerge for Production-Grade Agentic Systems**
A series of technical articles and tutorials are providing developers with blueprints for building robust, production-ready AI systems. Topics include building secure MCP servers with role-based access, designing AI agent sandbox architectures to safely execute code, and implementing reliable structured output pipelines to prevent JSON-related failures in AI workflows. (Source: Towards AI - Medium)

**Google Cloud Enhances BigQuery with Cross-Engine Iceberg Support**
Google Cloud has introduced new interoperability features for Apache Iceberg in BigQuery, including a serverless Iceberg REST catalog. This allows data teams to create, update, and query the same Iceberg tables across different engines like Spark, Flink, and Trino without duplicating data, simplifying modern data architectures. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)
