## AI News Digest

### Major Model and Platform Updates

**Anthropic Releases Claude Opus 4.7 with Performance Boosts and Higher Costs**
Anthropic has launched Claude Opus 4.7, an update to its flagship model that brings improved performance in coding, long-running tasks, and vision capabilities. However, the update includes a new tokenizer that can increase token counts for the same input by up to 35%, leading to potentially higher operational costs for developers, a detail that has sparked significant discussion. The model also introduces an "effort" parameter, allowing users to trade cost for more thorough reasoning on complex tasks. (Source: Towards AI - Medium, InfoQ, The New Stack)

**OpenAI Pushes into Specialized Domains with GPT-Rosalind and Upgraded Codex**
OpenAI has unveiled GPT-Rosalind, its first AI model specifically designed for the life sciences, aiming to accelerate drug discovery and genomics research by reasoning over complex biochemical data. Concurrently, the company has significantly upgraded Codex, its coding model, giving it more agentic capabilities and power over a user's desktop, positioning it as a direct competitor to tools from Anthropic and as a core component of a future OpenAI "superapp". (Source: MarkTechPost, TechCrunch, The New Stack)

**Google Expands Gemini and Gemma Model Families**
Google announced several major model updates, including Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS, a text-to-speech model focused on creating highly expressive and controllable AI voices in over 70 languages. The company also released Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6, enhancing embodied reasoning for physical robots, and open-sourced Gemma 4, a new series of open-weight models with multimodal capabilities and extended context windows. (Source: AI, MarkTechPost, InfoQ)

**New Open-Source Models Challenge Incumbents**
The Qwen Team has open-sourced Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, a powerful sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) vision-language model with 3 billion active parameters and strong agentic coding abilities. In a separate development, researchers from UCSD and Together AI introduced Parcae, a novel "looped" language model architecture that achieves the quality of a Transformer twice its size, offering a more efficient alternative for inference. (Source: MarkTechPost)

### The Rise of Agentic AI and Developer Tools

**OpenAI Agents SDK Adds Sandboxing for Safer Enterprise Deployment**
OpenAI has updated its Agents SDK to include a crucial new feature: sandboxed execution. This allows AI agents to operate within a controlled environment, separating the agent's "harness" from the underlying compute and mitigating risks, a key requirement for enterprises looking to deploy autonomous workflows in production safely. (Source: AI News, TechCrunch, The New Stack)

**Claude Code Evolves into a Proactive Software Engineering Partner**
Anthropic's Claude Code received several powerful updates, transforming it from a simple code assistant into an agentic partner. New features include an agent-based system for automatically reviewing pull requests, the ability to run "routines" overnight to complete complex tasks, and a redesigned desktop app. The model's power was demonstrated when a researcher used it to discover a 23-year-old, remotely exploitable vulnerability in the Linux kernel. (Source: InfoQ, The New Stack)

**The AI-Powered IDE Paradigm Shift**
The nature of software development tools is rapidly changing, with Anysphere releasing Cursor 3, a complete redesign that moves from a traditional file-editing IDE to an "agent-first" interface for managing parallel coding agents. This trend is further validated by the startup Factory, which raised $150 million at a $1.5 billion valuation to build autonomous AI coding tools for enterprises, competing directly with major labs. (Source: InfoQ, TechCrunch, WSJ)

**Enterprises Begin to Govern and Deploy AI Agents at Scale**
As agentic AI proliferates, major vendors are releasing tools for management and governance. AWS launched Agent Registry in preview, a centralized catalog for discovering and reusing AI agents across an organization. Meanwhile, SAP announced the integration of a network of AI agents into its SuccessFactors Human Capital Management (HCM) platform to automate administrative tasks in recruiting, payroll, and talent development. (Source: InfoQ, AI News)

**New Agentic Tools Empower Game and Design Creators**
The agentic trend is also reaching creative platforms. Roblox has equipped its AI assistant with new tools to help creators plan, build, and test games within its ecosystem. Similarly, design platform Canva has updated its AI assistant, enabling it to call various internal tools to generate complex, editable designs from a simple text prompt. (Source: TechCrunch)

This aligns with research from Google DeepMind, which released Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6, a model specifically designed to serve as the "cognitive brain" for robots by enhancing their embodied reasoning and ability to read instruments. (Source: TechCrunch, MarkTechPost)

Meanwhile, Apple's Mac Mini is facing a mysterious supply shortage, frustrating AI developers who favor the device for local model development. (Source: WSJ, TechCrunch)

In Southeast Asia, ride-hailing and delivery giant Grab is investing in AI-powered robots to automate meal delivery. (Source: AI News, WSJ)

### Global Economy &amp; Geopolitical Tensions

**Markets Rally on Iran Peace Hopes, but IMF Warns of Risks**
Global stock markets, including the tech-heavy Nasdaq, climbed to record highs on optimism surrounding a potential peace deal between the U.S. and Iran, which also caused oil prices to fall. Despite the market rally, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) issued a stark warning that a prolonged conflict could severely slash global growth, push government debt to match annual output by 2029, and cause a deep global downturn. European airlines are already facing potential jet fuel shortages within weeks if shipping through the Strait of Hormuz does not resume. (Source: WSJ.com: Markets, MarketWatch.com - Top Stories, NYT &gt; Business, WSJ.com: Economy)

**Trump Intensifies Pressure on Fed Chair Amid Political Battle**
President Trump has doubled down on threats to fire Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell if he doesn't resign, stating he will not drop a Justice Department criminal investigation into the chair. This has created a showdown, with Democrats making a final push to delay the confirmation hearing for Trump's proposed successor, Kevin M. Warsh. The situation highlights a legal ambiguity regarding what happens when a Fed chair's term expires without a confirmed replacement. (Source: WSJ.com: Economy, NYT &gt; Business)

**Former Treasury Secretary Urges Emergency Plan for Bond Market**
Former Treasury Secretary Henry Paulson warned that U.S. policymakers must prepare an emergency "break-the-glass" plan in case demand for U.S. Treasurys collapses. He cautioned that a crisis in the government bond market could trigger severe consequences across the entire economy. This comes as Treasury yields have been volatile, reacting to geopolitical news and domestic economic data. (Source: MarketWatch.com - Top Stories, WSJ.com: Markets)

**Key U.S. Economic Indicators Show Mixed Picture**
Recent economic data presents a varied outlook for the U.S. economy, with unemployment claims declining, suggesting a stable labor market, and the New York Fed survey showing an expansion in manufacturing. **China's Economy Shows Strength; Europe Faces Inflation**
China's GDP grew stronger than expected, driven by significant government spending on infrastructure projects like new rail lines, though a slide in housing prices has dampened consumer spending. (Source: NYT &gt; Business, WSJ.com: Economy)
