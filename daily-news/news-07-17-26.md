## AI News Digest

### Major Model &amp; Platform Releases
**Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3, a 2.8 Trillion Parameter Open Model**
Chinese AI lab Moonshot AI has released Kimi K3, a massive 2.8 trillion-parameter open Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model. The model, which activates 16 of its 896 experts per token, features a 1 million-token context window and is expected to challenge top-tier models from competitors like Anthropic, representing a significant step forward for China's AI capabilities. (Source: MarkTechPost, TechCrunch, WSJ, Towards AI)

**NVIDIA Unveils Nemotron 3 Embed for Advanced Retrieval**
NVIDIA released Nemotron 3 Embed, an open collection of embedding models designed for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). The 8-billion parameter checkpoint has achieved the #1 ranking on the RTEB benchmark, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance in helping AI agents find relevant information, a critical component for building more capable and accurate systems. (Source: Hugging Face - Blog, MarkTechPost)

**Thinking Machines Lab Debuts Multimodal 'Inkling' Model**
Thinking Machines Lab released its first open-weights model, Inkling, a 975 billion-parameter MoE transformer. The model is natively multimodal (text, image, audio), features a 1 million-token context window, and offers controllable "thinking effort," positioning it as a highly customizable foundation for developers rather than a direct competitor on pure performance benchmarks. (Source: MarkTechPost, TechCrunch, Wired)

**OpenAI Details GPT-Red, Its Internal AI Red-Teaming Tool**
OpenAI has shared details about GPT-Red, an internal AI model used for automated safety testing. Trained via self-play, GPT-Red significantly outperforms human red-teamers, winning 84% to 13% in a prompt injection challenge and discovering novel attack methods, which helped cut failures in a future model by 6x. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Soofi Consortium Releases Hybrid Mamba-Transformer Model**
The Soofi Consortium has released Soofi S 30B-A3B, an open foundation model for German and English. It uses a hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE architecture, showcasing ongoing innovation in model designs beyond pure transformers and providing a new option for bilingual applications. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**PrismML Ships Ultra-Efficient Bonsai 27B Models**
PrismML released Bonsai 27B, offering 1-bit and ternary (3-value) quantized versions of the Qwen3.6-27B model. These highly compressed models are designed to run on consumer hardware like laptops and phones, making powerful AI more accessible on edge devices. (Source: MarkTechPost)

Google has launched LiteRT.js, a JavaScript library that allows developers to run TensorFlow Lite (.tflite) models directly in the browser using WebGPU. In testimony to lawmakers, new Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh stated that the central bank has "no tolerance for persistently elevated inflation."
