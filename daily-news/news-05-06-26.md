## AI News Digest

### Major Model and Product Releases

**OpenAI Releases GPT-5.5 Instant as New Default ChatGPT Model**
OpenAI has rolled out GPT-5.5 Instant as the new default model for ChatGPT. The company claims the new model offers faster response times and reduced hallucinations, particularly in sensitive domains such as law, medicine, and finance, while maintaining the low latency of its predecessor. (Source: TechCrunch, The New Stack)

**Google Internally Tests "Remy," a Personal AI Agent for Gemini**
Google is reportedly testing a new AI personal agent named Remy within a staff-only version of its Gemini app. According to internal documents, Remy is designed to take actions on behalf of users to assist with both work-related and daily life tasks, signaling a deeper push into agentic AI. (Source: AI News)

**Anthropic Launches "Auto Mode" for Claude Code**
Anthropic has introduced an "auto mode" for its Claude Code product, enabling the AI to perform multi-step software development tasks autonomously. The system is designed with safety in mind, incorporating human approval checkpoints for sensitive operations like executing code or accessing files, while automating other parts of the workflow. (Source: InfoQ)

**Mistral Upgrades Le Chat with New Model and Agent Capabilities**
Mistral has released Mistral Medium 3.5, a 128-billion parameter model, and has integrated new agentic features into its Le Chat and Vibe products. These updates include a "work mode" and the ability to use remote agents, enhancing the platform's capacity for complex reasoning, instruction following, and coding tasks. (Source: InfoQ)

**Microsoft Introduces Agent 365 to Manage AI Armies**
Microsoft has launched Agent 365, a new platform designed to act as a "manager" for the growing number of AI agents operating within an enterprise environment. The tool aims to provide end-to-end observability and governance, helping security and IT teams manage risks associated with agents that can access data and invoke tools across various applications. (Source: Towards AI - Medium)

**Inworld AI Launches Realtime TTS-2 for More Natural AI Voices**
Inworld AI has released Realtime TTS-2, a "closed-loop" text-to-speech model for creating more realistic and adaptive AI agent voices. Unlike traditional TTS systems that rely only on text transcripts, this model conditions its output on the full audio context of a conversation, allowing it to adapt its tone and rhythm to how a user is actually speaking. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Mistral Aims to Close "Expressivity Gap" with Voxtral TTS**
Mistral has unveiled Voxtral TTS, a new multilingual voice cloning system that uses a hybrid architecture combining autoregressive and flow-matching techniques. The model is designed to overcome the flat, robotic sound of many TTS systems by better capturing the rhythm, emotion, and unique characteristics of a speaker's voice. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Google Speeds Up Gemma 4 Inference by 3x with MTP Drafters**
Google AI has released Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) drafters for its Gemma 4 family of models. This new technique uses speculative decoding to allow the model to predict multiple tokens at once, reportedly delivering up to a 3x increase in inference speed without a loss in output quality. (Source: MarkTechPost)
