## AI News Digest

### Major Model &amp; Framework Releases

**Google Launches Veo 3.1 Lite for Accessible Video Generation**
Google has released Veo 3.1 Lite, a cost-effective and high-speed tier of its generative video model, available via the Gemini API. The release aims to make production-scale video generation more feasible for developers by significantly lowering the price per second of generated content, which has been a major barrier to adoption. (Source: MarkTechPost, AI)

**Alibaba Unveils Qwen3.5 Omni as a Native Multimodal Competitor**
The Alibaba Qwen team released Qwen3.5-Omni, a native "omnimodal" model designed to process text, audio, and video in an end-to-end architecture. Positioned as a direct competitor to models like Gemini 3.1 Pro, it represents a shift away from "wrapper" models that stitch separate encoders onto a text backbone. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Microsoft Releases SOTA Multilingual Embedding Models**
Microsoft AI has launched Harrier-OSS-v1, a new family of three open-source multilingual text embedding models (270M, 0.6B, and 27B parameters). The models achieved state-of-the-art results on the Multilingual MTEB v2 benchmark, providing high-quality semantic representations across a wide range of languages. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Hugging Face Announces Production-Ready TRL v1.0**
Hugging Face has officially released TRL (Transformer Reinforcement Learning) v1.0, transitioning the library into a stable, production-ready framework. TRL v1.0 unifies the post-training pipeline, offering a standardized API for Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), Reward Modeling, and alignment techniques like DPO. (Source: Hugging Face - Blog, MarkTechPost)

**Liquid AI Challenges Scaling Laws with Compact LFM2.5-350M Model**
Liquid AI has released LFM2.5-350M, a compact 350-million-parameter model that was pre-trained on an immense 28 trillion tokens. The model aims to demonstrate that "intelligence density" through extensive training and reinforcement learning can produce powerful results without relying on massive parameter counts. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**IBM Releases Granite 4.0 Vision for Enterprise Document Understanding**
IBM has introduced Granite 4.0 3B Vision, a compact 3-billion-parameter multimodal model optimized for enterprise document processing. The model is designed to handle complex document-related tasks, combining visual and textual understanding for business applications. (Source: Hugging Face - Blog)

**Caltech Researchers Claim Breakthrough in AI Model Compression**
A research team at the California Institute of Technology reports creating a large language model that radically compresses its size without a corresponding loss in performance. This development could have significant implications for deploying powerful AI on devices with limited computational resources. (Source: Technology - WSJ.com)

**TII Releases Falcon Perception Model**
The Technology Innovation Institute (TII) of the UAE has released Falcon Perception, a new multimodal model. This addition to the Falcon family of models focuses on perception capabilities, integrating vision and language understanding. (Source: Hugging Face - Blog)

### AI Infrastructure, Tools, and Development

**Amazon-Affiliated Researchers Launch A-Evolve for Agent Automation**
Researchers have released A-Evolve, a framework designed to automate the development and improvement of autonomous AI agents. Described as a potential "PyTorch moment" for agentic AI, it replaces manual tuning with a systematic process of automated state mutation and self-correction based on performance benchmarks. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Salesforce VoiceAgentRAG Drastically Cuts Voice AI Latency**
Salesforce AI Research has introduced VoiceAgentRAG, a dual-agent memory router architecture that reduces retrieval latency in voice-based RAG systems by a factor of 316x. This innovation aims to make voice AI interactions feel more natural by keeping response times within the crucial 200ms conversational budget. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Ollama Leverages Apple's MLX for Faster Local AI on Macs**
The Ollama platform has been updated to use Apple's MLX framework, enabling large language models to run significantly faster and more efficiently on Mac computers. This move makes local AI development and execution more practical for users of Apple silicon. (Source: The New Stack)

**Runway Launches $10M Fund to Support AI Video Startups**
AI video generation company Runway has launched a $10 million fund and a "Builders" program to invest in and support early-stage startups building applications with its models. The initiative signals Runway's push toward more interactive, real-time "video intelligence" use cases. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**Qodo Raises $70M for AI Code Verification**
As AI coding assistants flood the software development world with generated code, startup Qodo has raised $70 million to focus on code verification. The company is betting that the critical challenge will not be generating code, but ensuring that it is correct, secure, and functional. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**Agent-Infra Releases AIO Sandbox for Secure Agent Execution**
Agent-Infra has launched the AIO Sandbox, an open-source, all-in-one runtime environment for AI agents. The sandbox provides an isolated environment with a browser, shell, and shared filesystem, addressing the infrastructure challenge of safely executing agent-generated code and plans. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Google Introduces AppFunctions to Make Android "Agent-First"**
Google has unveiled AppFunctions, an early beta feature for Android that allows AI agents and assistants to use functionalities from different apps as building blocks. This move is part of a broader strategy to transform Android into an "agent-first" operating system where users can accomplish complex tasks through AI orchestration. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

### Security, Governance, and Ethical Concerns

**LiteLLM Suffers Major Supply Chain Attack via PyPI**
The popular open-source AI gateway LiteLLM was the target of a supply chain attack, where a malicious version was uploaded to the Python Package Index (PyPI). The compromised package, downloaded over 40,000 times, contained malware designed to exfiltrate sensitive information like API keys. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering, AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**AI Recruiting Startup Mercor Hit by Cyberattack Linked to LiteLLM**
Following the LiteLLM compromise, AI recruiting startup Mercor confirmed it suffered a security incident. An extortion-focused hacking group has taken credit for the attack, claiming to have stolen company data by exploiting the vulnerability. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**OpenAI Shuts Down Public Sora Tool, Sparking Speculation**

**IRS Testing Palantir AI for Auditing and Fraud Detection**

### Scientific and Medical AI Research

**MIT Researchers Use AI to Uncover Atomic Defects in Materials**
A new AI model developed at MIT can identify and measure atomic-level defects in materials. Understanding these defects is crucial as they can be leveraged to improve desirable properties like mechanical strength, heat transfer, and energy-conversion efficiency. (Source: MIT News - Artificial intelligence)

**Meta's TRIBE v2 Model Predicts Human Brain Activity**
Meta AI has developed TRIBE v2, a trimodal AI model trained on over 1,100 hours of brain scans from 720 subjects. The model can simulate and predict human brain activity, potentially allowing researchers to conduct virtual neuroscience experiments and accelerate the pace of discovery. (Source: Towards AI - Medium)

**Mantis Biotech Creates 'Digital Twins' of Humans for Medical Research**
Startup Mantis Biotech is using AI to create synthetic datasets that function as "digital twins" of the human body. By combining disparate sources of data to represent anatomy and physiology, the company aims to solve the problem of data scarcity in medical research and drug development. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**Eli Lilly and InSilico Medicine Sign Major AI Drug Discovery Deal**
Pharmaceutical giant Eli Lilly has entered into a drug discovery partnership with AI firm InSilico Medicine. The deal, which could be valued at up to $2.75 billion, underscores the increasing reliance of major pharmaceutical companies on AI to identify and develop new therapeutic targets. (Source: Technology - WSJ.com)
