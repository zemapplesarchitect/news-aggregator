Amid escalating tensions, Iran has issued a threat to target U.S.-linked AI data centers with missile strikes. Cities and states are beginning to push back against the construction of new data centers due to their immense demand for electricity and significant environmental impact. **The Urgent Need for AI Agent Governance**
As AI systems evolve from simple chatbots into autonomous agents that can plan, make decisions, and execute tasks with limited human oversight, the need for robust governance is becoming a critical priority. Microsoft's terms of use for its Copilot AI assistant include a disclaimer that the service is intended "for entertainment purposes only," advising users not to rely on its outputs. **MIT Researchers Boost Data Center Efficiency with AI Workload Balancing**
A new system developed by MIT researchers intelligently balances workloads to improve the performance and efficiency of flash storage hardware in data centers. (Source: MIT News - Artificial intelligence)

**'MaxToki' AI Model Predicts Cellular Aging**
Researchers have developed an AI model named MaxToki that can predict how individual cells will age by analyzing their gene activity at a single point in time. Unlike previous models that view cells as static snapshots, MaxToki can forecast their future states, offering a powerful new tool for biological and longevity research. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Anthropic Designs Three-Agent System for Complex AI Development**
Anthropic has detailed a new "three-agent harness" designed to improve the reliability of AI in long-running, complex tasks like full-stack software development. The system separates responsibilities into a planner agent, a generator agent, and an evaluation agent, creating a structured, iterative workflow that helps maintain coherence and quality over multi-hour coding sessions. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Benchmark Shows Dynamic Languages are Faster and Cheaper for AI Code Generation**
A comprehensive benchmark testing Anthropic's Claude Code across 13 programming languages found that dynamically typed languages like Ruby, Python, and JavaScript produced correct code faster and more cheaply than statically typed languages. The results challenge common assumptions, suggesting that for current AI code generation tasks, the verbosity of static types can increase costs and token counts without a proportional gain in quality. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)
